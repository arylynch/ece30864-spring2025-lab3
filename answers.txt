pros and cons of agile 

pros: It makes teams more resilient to scope changes in the project
      It breaks down a project into smaller, several stages
cons: There can be a lack of documentation because it is not as important
      There is less predictability


roles / responsibilties

scrum master role: All scrum related tasks – JIRA tooling, facilitating sprints and meetings, standups all done by the Scrum Master
product owner: Sets direction for the team, handles release management and stakeholder management
